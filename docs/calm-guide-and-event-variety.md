# A calmer introduction and varied gatherings

## Tutorial

Start a new game with First steps enabled, or press H during play to replay the guide. Each step begins with a first-person explanation from the returning neighbor, with their portrait and a World paused label. Enter, Space or the Let me try button resumes real practice. Completing the action pauses for the next explanation. T or Skip ends the guide; Esc opens the existing safe pause menu. The guide grants no money or upgrades.

Movement, trash spawning, NPCs, janitors, rent and owner timers freeze during explanations. Explanations and practice state survive saving and Continue. Existing active tutorials show their current explanation once on loading. The opening's disable-guide toggle still works.

## Owner timing

The first three projects retain their three-minute waits. Completing the third project, and every later recurring favor, independently draws that owner's next delay from 180–600 seconds. This is active gameplay time: menus, pause and tutorial explanations freeze it. The remaining time is saved, and Continue does not draw another delay. Owners remain ready until visited; requests have no deadlines.

## Four different gatherings

Use J → Mall life as before. Events rotate within each court, keeping the existing eligibility checks, rewards and five-minute gathering cooldown.

| Gathering | Activity | Preferred location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Book swap | Listen to three readers and match their books | Western upper court |
| Cafe tasting | Brew tea, toast cinnamon bread, then plate berry cake | Eastern upper court |
| Plant sale | Visit three scattered beds and hold E for two seconds at each | Western lower court |
| Makers market | Find donated paper, ribbon and frames at three separate markers | Eastern lower court |

The hosting point varies on repeat visits and is checked for clear, reachable space. Plant beds and makers' materials have distinct pixel props and green journal-map markers. Physical events cannot be completed by clicking answers at the table: return to the court, finish the three stops, then collect the reward at the table. Completed steps and exact locations save and reload. Old active gatherings retain their original matching interactions until completed.

## Story boards

New games position each chapter board differently within its court. Completed boards show a reading shelf, record, handmade lantern or supper table matching that court's contribution. Existing checkpoints retain their original story layout and keepsake locations; no unfound discoveries move when continuing or unlocking another court. Storefront lanterns retain the accepted design.

## Playtest

- At 800×600, start with the guide on. Hold movement or E during an explanation; nothing advances. Use Enter to practice, T to skip, or H to replay.
- Complete the third owner project, inspect its countdown in J, save and Continue. Verify its remaining wait survives. Developer mode can prepare improved stores quickly.
- Use F3 → 7 in developer mode to prepare a gathering; finish it and try another court's event. For plants and makers, inspect J's map, follow the markers, then return to the table.
- Save after the first physical event stop and Continue. The same two remaining locations should be visible.
- Use F3 → 6 to inspect the story boards before and after chapter completion.
