# Northgate: Abandoned Mall Restorer — First Playable Preview

Welcome to Northgate. Restore an abandoned mall, bring its neighbors back, and help revive the winter lantern festival.

## In this first release

- Four playable courts with forty businesses to reopen.
- Collect litter, clean floors, sell recycling and invest in tools and welcoming scenery.
- Hire a janitor in each court, with walking and cleaning upgrades.
- Shoppers visit stores, carry purchases, rest at benches and gather with friends.
- Meet shop owners, complete their first three projects and help with twelve recurring favors. Later requests arrive after independent 3–10 minute waits.
- A four-chapter lantern festival story with scattered keepsakes, neighbor conversations and themed completed boards.
- Four community gatherings: book swaps, cafe preparation, scattered planting and donated-material hunts.
- A skippable tutorial with paused explanations from your character.
- Autosave, Continue, backup recovery and separate developer saves.
- Pixel art, quiet chiptune music, fullscreen/windowed play and a safe pause/exit screen.

## Download and play

Choose the archive for your computer:

- **Windows:** `MallRestorer-windows-*.zip` (64-bit).
- **macOS:** `MallRestorer-darwin-*.tar.gz` (Apple silicon).
- **Linux:** `MallRestorer-linux-*.tar.gz` (64-bit, Ubuntu 22.04 or compatible).

Extract the archive, then launch `MallRestorer` (`MallRestorer.exe` on Windows). Keep the `_internal` folder beside the executable. Python is not required. Each download includes a README; matching `.sha256` files let you verify the archive.

**Controls:** WASD/arrows to move, E to interact, Hold E for work, J for the journal, H for the guide, T to skip the guide, M to mute, F11 for fullscreen/windowed, and Esc to pause. Existing saves from the approved playtest build remain compatible.

## Preview status

This is an early playable release, marked as a pre-release while mechanics, balance and story develop. Windows and macOS builds are unsigned; Intel Mac builds are not included. Save backup recovery is available, but keeping a personal copy before trying developer builds is useful.
