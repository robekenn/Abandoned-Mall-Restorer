# Northgate: Abandoned Mall Restorer — v0.1.1

This preview update addresses playtester feedback on controls, quests, shopping and NPCs.

## Changes

- Left-click nearby trash or dirt to collect it. Tool reach and bag capacity still apply.
- Pickup quests count litter collected in any open court and choose a random target of 3–10 pieces. The target and progress persist through Continue; existing pickup quests keep their original five-piece target.
- F2 or Pause → Settings opens separate music/effects sliders, fullscreen, guide and favor reminder options, and twelve remappable controls. Settings persist separately from game progress.
- Clicking an upgrade row inspects it; Purchase or Enter/Space buys it. Equipment shops have clearer UPGRADES signs and map colors, and tool reach uses game tiles.
- Nearby work objects take priority over ordinary shopper conversations. Active favors show their store and owner and mark the doorway and map. Available favors have quieter reminders.
- Janitors reconsider closer reachable litter while walking and leave enough litter across the mall for your active pickup quest.
- Speech bubbles stay with their speakers and disappear offscreen. North-facing owners' bubbles leave quest worktables visible.

## Download and play

Choose the Windows x64 ZIP, Linux x64 tar.gz, or Apple-silicon macOS tar.gz. Extract the archive and launch `MallRestorer` (`MallRestorer.exe` on Windows), keeping the `_internal` folder beside it. Python is not required. Matching `.sha256` files verify each archive.

Your v0.1.0 save remains compatible. Continue resumes it, and New game keeps your settings. Controls default to WASD/arrows for movement, E for interactions, left-click for litter, J for the journal, F2 for settings, H for the guide, T to skip it, M to mute, F5 to save, F11 for fullscreen/windowed, and Esc to pause.

This remains an early playable preview. Windows and macOS downloads are unsigned; Intel Mac builds are not included. Report bugs through the repository's Issues page with your operating system and what you were doing.
