# A place worth staying in

Northgate's restored courts now offer something to do between cleanup and shop openings.

## Visitors

Visitors still arrive and leave through the west main entrance. They enter actual store doors, stay inside briefly, and emerge with shopping bags. On floors at least 75% clean, some visitors browse a second reopened shop in the same court. Purchased benches offer seated rests; restored fountains invite a longer pause. Visitors can stop beside a friend or join a community table. Chatting pauses the speaker and uses the existing speech bubbles.

Seats are reserved while someone approaches them. The seated pose is drawn on the bench while its navigation point remains safely in front of the furniture. Visitor paths refresh when a community table changes the obstacles; neither customers nor janitors should cut through it.

## A conversation, not another job

At a reopened regular store, press **E**, then **C / Just chat**. Mara, Jules, Iris, Theo, Remy, Fern, Wren and Em have individual three-stage memories; the other owners discuss their own supplies and returning neighbors. Conversation stages follow the store's earned improvements. Alternating chats react to the court's cleanliness or share an everyday observation. There is no reward to farm, and chatting does not accept or consume an owner request. The owner appears beside the shop door while speaking.

## Community tables

Open **J → Mall life** with **4**, choose a court with Up/Down, then press Enter or click its card to host. A court needs two reopened rent-paying shops and at least 50% local cleanliness. One gathering can be underway at a time. The event table appears on a reachable clear floor spot in a theme-specific part of that court, marked in gold on the journal map.

| Gathering | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| Neighborhood book swap | Find a story or handbook that suits a neighbor's memory or plans |
| Taste of Northgate | Prepare tea, cinnamon toast and berry cake in order |
| Little plant sale | Plant three scattered community beds with Hold E |
| Evening makers market | Find three donated materials around the court |

Press **E** at the table for instructions. Books and cafe preparation use **1/2/3**, arrows + Enter, or a click. Plants and makers send you to three green map markers; return to the table after completing them. Incorrect choices receive a gentle hint and can be retried. After helping three neighbors, collect the thank-you payment. North/East/Garden/Commons pay $200/$500/$1,000/$1,800 respectively. The next event rotates and waits five minutes of active play. There is no deadline, entry fee, or restoration gate tied to gatherings.

Completed events, cooldowns, the current table and conversation progress save automatically alongside the existing version-one checkpoint. Older checkpoints default to no active event. Ordinary transient shoppers still return through the entrance when reloading.

## Pause and exit

**Esc** opens the pause screen. Resume, save progress, or choose Exit game. Exiting requires confirmation, with **Stay in Northgate** selected by default. Window close opens the same confirmation. Esc backs out safely; it still closes other menus first. Movement, cleaning, rent, owner timers, events and autosave timers freeze while paused. A failed exit save keeps the game open, allowing another attempt or a deliberate exit without saving. Closing the opening screen does not overwrite an existing Continue slot.

## Review

Launch `python main.py --dev --windowed` (Windows: `py main.py --dev --windowed`). **F3 → 7** prepares a gathering without advancing story chapters. Close F3, approach its gold marker and try a wrong answer, then finish all three conversations. Use C at Pages Bookshop for Mara's memory. Reopen and purchase a bench or fountain to watch visitors linger. Hire a janitor and watch dirt use a broom and litter use a grabber. Try Esc twice and the window close button before confirming an exit.

The 16×24 character sprites retain their original 48×72 display size and ground footprints. They now use softer head and shoulder outlines, shaded clothing, clearer boots and distinct hairstyles. Cleaning has four coordinated reaching and tool frames for both the worker and janitors. The existing pixel palette, lanterns and storefront perspective are preserved.
