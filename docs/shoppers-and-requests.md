# Small actions, familiar faces

The mall now fills with visitors as businesses reopen. Owner requests give you new things to do and let you earn shop upgrades through helping people.

## Returning shoppers

Visitors arrive about every eight seconds when there is space in the population. Reopened shops attract them; at least 50% cleanliness and purchased benches/fountains support more visitors, up to twelve at once. They walk up to an open shop, enter through its briefly opened door, spend 10–14 seconds inside, then walk back out. Shoppers inside are hidden and cannot be greeted. They may linger at a restored amenity before leaving. Visitors walk around solid obstacles and only enter unlocked galleries; only a shop’s own narrow door passage allows a visitor to cross into that shop. They do not block the player, change rent on their own, or create extra litter beyond the existing litter timer.

Press E near a visitor to say hello. They have names, short remarks and four animated clothing variants. During a welcome request, greeting the same person repeatedly counts once; departed visitors keep their counted greeting. New visitors continue arriving, so the request cannot run out of people.

Northgate Supplies and Eastgate Workshop keep their equipment menus and do not give owner requests. Each owner offers three projects in order. The first becomes available after three minutes of play with that shop open. Each claimed improvement starts a fresh three-minute wait before its next request, so requests cannot be completed back to back. Timers are independent per shop and stop while menus or the journal are open. Closed shops do not accumulate readiness. An available request stays available until accepted. Accept with Enter, Space or a click; Esc/E closes the conversation. Menus pause movement, litter, visitors, request work and rent. M still mutes music and effects.

## Earn the improvements

| Project | Work | Permanent improvement | Added base rent / 5s | Cash thank-you |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A fresh start | Collect a delivery at the blue crate marker by a section bin; return to the owner | Welcoming display | $0.5 | $50 |
| Room to grow | Stand at each blue seedling marker and hold E for 1.5 seconds; return to the owner | Community corner | $1 | $100 |
| Hello, neighbors | Hold E for 2 seconds at the welcome-sign marker and greet three different visitors; return to the owner | Neighborhood favorite | $2 | $200 |

Delivery supplies use a separate satchel; they do not take trash capacity or sell at bins. Watering and sign setup need continuous E while standing still and within reach. Releasing E or moving restarts that short interaction. A progress bar shows the work. Community trays are temporary request props and do not grant free purchased planter upgrades.

The single HUD objective shows your next action. Press J to see the mall map, detailed income/equipment stats and every owner’s readiness or countdown. Blue dots in the journal map mark work sites; when ready, they mark the owner to return to. A ready request does not pay automatically: visit its owner and choose Claim improvement. Each improvement and cash reward is granted once. The shop gains displays, flower boxes and pennants as its projects finish.

One request is active at a time. There are no deadlines, required purchases or penalties. Other owners let you finish your current task first. Ordinary cleanup, upgrades and section unlocks stay available while helping an owner. Each store earns up to $3.5 additional base rent; the existing mall-cleanliness tiers apply to this income.

## Review checklist

1. Reopen a store and watch a shopper enter, disappear inside, exit through the door and leave. Purchase a bench or fountain and watch visitors linger.
2. Wait three minutes of play after reopening Pages; meet Mara and accept the delivery, collect supplies with a full trash bag, then return and claim. Verify $50 and +$0.5 base rent once.
3. After claiming the delivery, verify Mara cannot give another request until three more minutes of play. Then water both trays. Release E or move midway and check the progress resets. Verify no free mall planter purchase.
4. Set up the welcome sign and greet three different shoppers. Repeatedly greeting one shopper must not finish the request.
5. Claim all three improvements. Check the storefront decorations, +$3.5 total base rent and no fourth project.
6. Open the east gallery and verify Remy/Ada use east work sites. Check that shops, benches, fountains and sealed galleries keep safe walking routes.
7. Check both owner conversations and the tracker at 800x600, mouse/keyboard controls, journal pause, countdowns, J/Esc and M mute.

Progress still resets when the game closes, as before.
