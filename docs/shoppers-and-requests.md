# Small actions, familiar faces

The mall now fills with visitors as businesses reopen. Owner requests give you new things to do and let you earn shop upgrades through helping people.

## Returning shoppers

Visitors arrive about every eight seconds when there is space in the population. Reopened shops attract them; at least 50% cleanliness and purchased benches/fountains support more visitors, up to twelve at once in the first two areas, sixteen with Garden Arcade open and twenty with Community Commons open. They walk up to an open shop, enter through its briefly opened door, spend 10–14 seconds inside, then walk back out. Shoppers inside are hidden and cannot be greeted. They may linger at a restored amenity before leaving. Visitors walk around solid obstacles and only enter unlocked galleries; only a shop’s own narrow door passage allows a visitor to cross into that shop. They do not block the player, change rent on their own, or create extra litter beyond the existing litter timer.

The opened door leaf sits on the right-hand hinge. Press E near a visitor to say hello. Their speech bubble stays above their head and they briefly stop walking. They have names, short remarks and four animated clothing variants. During a welcome request, greeting the same person repeatedly counts once; departed visitors keep their counted greeting. New visitors continue arriving, so the request cannot run out of people.

Garden Arcade and Community Commons each have nine owners. Every area has its own signed delivery station.

Northgate Supplies and Eastgate Workshop keep their equipment menus and do not give owner requests. Each owner offers three initial projects in order, then twelve rotating repeatable favors. The first becomes available after three minutes of play with that shop open. Each claimed improvement starts a fresh three-minute wait before its next request, so requests cannot be completed back to back. Timers are independent per shop and stop while menus or the journal are open. Closed shops do not accumulate readiness. An available request stays available until accepted. Accept with Enter, Space or a click; Esc/E closes the conversation. Menus pause movement, litter, visitors, request work and rent. M still mutes music and effects.

## Earn the improvements

| Project | Work | Permanent improvement | Added base rent / 5s | Cash thank-you |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A fresh start | Collect supplies from the signed delivery station in that section; return to the owner | Welcoming display | $0.5 | $50 |
| A window worth stopping for | Press E at the blue worktable; arrange three shop-specific products to match the shelf plan, then return to the owner | Curated window | $1 | $100 |
| Hello, neighbors | Hold E for 2 seconds at the welcome-sign marker and greet three different visitors; return to the owner | Neighborhood favorite | $2 | $200 |

Delivery supplies use a separate satchel; they do not take trash capacity or sell at bins. The north pickup station is near the southwest entrance; the east station is beside the southeast wall. Both have a raised platform, stacked packages and a DELIVERIES sign, visible before any request. Active deliveries mark the collection point in blue on the J-key map. Supplies no longer appear on arbitrary floor tiles near trash bins.

For the second request, press E at the blue worktable near the owner’s storefront. The owner’s shelf plan stays visible: choose products from left to right using keys 1–3 or by clicking. Backspace undoes your last choice. An incorrect arrangement resets the choices without charging money; Esc/E leaves the task unfinished so you can return later. Each store has different products. Completing the matching arrangement prepares a curated window; return to the owner to claim its improvement. Holding E cannot skip the puzzle. The puzzle pauses all game systems, just like other menus.

The third request still uses continuous E while standing still to set up the welcome sign, then greetings with three distinct shoppers. Releasing E or moving restarts the short sign interaction. A progress bar shows the work. Purchased mall fixtures stay separate from all owner rewards.

The single HUD objective shows your next action. Press J to see the mall map, detailed income/equipment stats and every owner’s readiness or countdown. Blue dots in the journal map mark work sites; when ready, they mark the owner to return to. A ready request does not pay automatically: visit its owner and choose Claim improvement. Each improvement and cash reward is granted once. The shop gains displays, a curated product window and pennants as its projects finish.

One request is active at a time. There are no deadlines, required purchases or penalties. Other owners let you finish your current task first. Ordinary cleanup, upgrades and section unlocks stay available while helping an owner. The three initial projects earn $3.5 additional base rent; recurring favors can add further $0.50 increases; the existing mall-cleanliness tiers apply to this income.

See [community favors and expansion](community-favors-and-expansion.md) for the full repeatable catalog and later areas.

## Review checklist

1. Reopen a store and watch a shopper enter, disappear inside, exit through the door and leave. Purchase a bench or fountain and watch visitors linger.
2. Wait three minutes of play after reopening Pages; meet Mara and accept the delivery, collect supplies with a full trash bag, then return and claim. Verify $50 and +$0.5 base rent once.
3. After claiming the delivery, verify Mara cannot give another request until three more minutes of play. Then arrange the window display. Test undo, wrong order, retry, mouse/keyboard controls and leaving/reopening. Verify no free mall fixture purchase.
4. Set up the welcome sign and greet three different shoppers. Repeatedly greeting one shopper must not finish the request.
5. Claim all three improvements. Check the storefront decorations, +$3.5 total base rent and a recurring favor available after the next three-minute wait.
6. Open the east gallery and verify Remy/Ada use east work sites. Check that shops, benches, fountains and sealed galleries keep safe walking routes.
7. Check both owner conversations and the tracker at 800x600, mouse/keyboard controls, journal pause, countdowns, J/Esc and M mute.

Owner requests, cooldowns and rewards now persist in checkpoints. See [saving and story](saving-and-lantern-story.md).
